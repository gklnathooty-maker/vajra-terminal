VAJRA TERMINAL - PORTABLE PENDRIVE (USB) PLUG & PLAY EDITION
================================================================================

HOW TO COPY TO YOUR PENDRIVE:
1. Insert your USB pendrive into this computer.
2. Copy this ENTIRE folder ("New folder" or rename it to "VajraTerminal") 
   onto your pendrive.
   (Make sure the 'runtime' and 'node_modules' folders are copied along with it).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE ON ANY OTHER COMPUTER:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Plug the pendrive into ANY Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC (no installation, no Python, 
and no Node.js required!):

METHOD 1: INSTITUTIONAL QUANTITATIVE WEB WORKSTATION (RECOMMENDED)
   - Double-click: "LAUNCH-FROM-PENDRIVE.bat" (or "VAJRA-TERMINAL.bat")
   - What happens: It automatically runs the self-contained portable engine 
     from the pendrive and opens the full institutional workstation window in 
     frameless desktop mode.
   - Live Prices: Automatically updates every 60 seconds with live NSE/BSE quotes.
   - When finished: Double-click "STOP-TERMINAL.bat" to shut down the engine 
     before safely unplugging your pendrive.

METHOD 2: STANDALONE PYTHON GUI EXECUTABLE
   - Double-click: "Vajra-Terminal-GUI.exe"
   - What happens: Launches the native standalone Python desktop application 
     directly from the pendrive with zero dependencies.
